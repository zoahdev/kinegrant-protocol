# Code of conduct

Project participation requires respectful, technical, good-faith collaboration.
Harassment, threats, discrimination, disclosure of private information, and
deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

Security criticism is welcome and should be evaluated on evidence. Maintainers
may remove content or restrict participation when needed to protect contributors
or the project. Serious conduct concerns can be reported through the private
contact method listed at <https://kinegrant.com> when one becomes available.
