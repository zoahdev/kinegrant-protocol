# KineGrant Physical Action Vocabulary

> Status: v0.2 draft · normative within the `kg.action.*` namespace

The vocabulary gives robots, policy issuers, and auditors one machine-readable
name for each physical action. Requests, policy rules, capabilities, and
receipts all refer to these terms. A term is strict: if an adapter cannot map a
legacy string to a canonical term, the request is rejected rather than
widened.

## Canonical terms

| Term | Category | Risk tier | Data-sensitive | Description |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| `kg.action.observe` | sense | 1 | no | Sensing a target without persisting or transmitting the observation. |
| `kg.action.record` | data | 2 | yes | Persisting an observation for a stated purpose. |
| `kg.action.touch` | manipulate | 2 | no | Making reversible contact with a target. |
| `kg.action.grasp` | manipulate | 3 | no | Securely gripping and possibly lifting a target. |
| `kg.action.move` | transform | 3 | no | Changing the position or configuration of a target or of the robot itself. |
| `kg.action.open` | access | 2 | no | Opening a barrier, door, lid, or physical lock. |
| `kg.action.enter` | access | 3 | no | Passing a robot or carried item through a boundary. |
| `kg.action.retain` | transform | 2 | no | Keeping possession of a target after the immediate task. |
| `kg.action.train_on_data` | data | 4 | yes | Using observations to train or improve an AI model. |

Risk tiers are advisory ordering hints for policy writers, not a replacement
for site-specific risk assessment:

- 1: observation that does not persist or transmit data;
- 2: reversible or low-impact actions;
- 3: actions that can damage objects or require supervision;
- 4: high-impact actions such as training on recorded observations;
- 5: reserved for the highest-impact actions (none defined in draft v0.2).

## Rules

1. Every request, allow rule, deny rule, capability, and receipt action field
   uses a canonical `kg.action.*` term in strict deployments.
2. Unknown terms fail closed when `require_known_actions` is enabled on the
   policy engine. The engine returns `unknown_action` for requests and rejects
   rules containing unknown terms at construction time.
3. Adapters map legacy strings (`open`, `record`, `train_on_data`) to
   canonical terms; the mapping is explicit and never wildcard-expands.
4. `data_sensitivity: true` marks actions that touch observations of people or
   spaces; policies for these actions should demand a purpose and, where
   appropriate, `requires_human_present` or `required_context`.

## Machine-readable registry

The full registry is published as
[`schemas/action-vocabulary.schema.json`](schemas/action-vocabulary.schema.json)
and generated by `kinegrant.vocabulary.registry()` in the reference
implementation. Policy tooling and the public verifier can validate a
vocabulary snapshot against the schema without trusting the website.
