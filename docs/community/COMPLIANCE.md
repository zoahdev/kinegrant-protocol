# Community Compliance Boundaries

> These are community guardrails, not legal advice. When in doubt, consult a
> qualified lawyer in your jurisdiction.

## Why this exists

The KineGrant community operates openly and internationally. To keep
participation safe for everyone — including contributors in jurisdictions with
strict financial regulation — the community deliberately avoids anything that
could be construed as a financial scheme or unsolicited outreach.

## Boundaries (do)

- Open-source collaboration under Apache-2.0.
- Public decision records, RFCs, and contribution credentials.
- Non-financial recognition: reputation, credits, and public records.
- Standards outreach: submitting to recognized standards bodies and public
  communities (for example ODRL and IEEE) through official channels.
- Grants or donations only through a future, separately established legal
  entity or recognized foundation, never through the community itself, and
  never through automated outreach.

## Boundaries (do not)

- Do not issue, promise, or reference tokens, coins, NFTs, points, or any
  tradable instrument.
- Do not raise funds, collect membership fees, or accept investments.
- Do not promise or imply returns, profits, equity, or ownership units.
- Do not send bulk, automated, or cold-email outreach; do not maintain
  recipient campaigns or mailing-list automation in this repository.
- Do not register or claim to be a legal entity, association, or "DAO" in a
  way that implies organizational status.
- Do not make certification, safety, or standards-recognition claims.
- Do not let "founding slots" or credentials carry financial meaning.

## Enforcement

Proposals, workflows, or marketing that would cross these boundaries are
rejected without a community vote and removed from the repository.
