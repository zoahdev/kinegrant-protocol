# Interim Steering Committee — Seat Nomination and Selection

> Companion to Discussion #246 and the governance charter
> (`GOVERNANCE.md` + `docs/community/CHARTER.md`). Status: open for nominations.

## Purpose

The project is moving from a single-maintainer project toward neutral,
community-governed operation. The interim steering committee is a small,
public, rotating body that resolves escalations and runs RFC final votes.
This document defines how the first external seats are filled.

## Seats

- **2–3 external seats** in addition to the interim chair.
- Preferred backgrounds (one or more): independent implementation author,
  security/robotics security, protocol/standards, hardware, or open-source
  governance.
- Seats are **public, rotating, and never sold**. They carry no equity,
  token, compensation, or economic benefit.

## Eligibility

Anyone may nominate themselves or another person if they:

- have engaged with KineGrant publicly (issue, discussion, PR, reproduction,
  or independent implementation), **or** bring relevant expertise from
  physical-AI security, robotics, standards, or governance;
- disclose any conflict of interest (employer, funding, competing protocol,
  or personal stake);
- commit to the charter's hard non-goals: no tokens, no fundraising through
  the community, no legal-entity claim, no certification claims.

## Nomination and selection

1. **Nominate**: reply to Discussion #246 (or open an issue) with name/handle,
   background, and conflict-of-interest disclosure.
2. **Confirm interest**: the nominee confirms they accept a public, unpaid,
   rotating seat.
3. **Review**: the interim chair and current members review eligibility and
   conflicts; objections are recorded publicly.
4. **Appoint**: the seat is announced in the decision log
   (`docs/community/DECISION-LOG.md`), with the rotation term.

## Rotation and removal

- Terms are staggered (e.g., 6–12 months) so the committee never fully turns
  over at once.
- A member may resign at any time; resignation is recorded publicly.
- Removal requires a supermajority vote of the remaining members, with the
  reason recorded.

## First priority

The first external seat should go to an author of a non-reference
implementation (per KGP-RFC-0004) or to an independent security reviewer —
the two groups that most directly increase protocol trust.
